Convertible note activity lesson plan



# Convertible note vibe coding

## Aim:

1. Startups should understand the terms discount and cap
2. Startups should understand the risks of future raise valuation on their SAFE agreements
3. Startups should understand future valuation can be in and out of their control
4. Startups should understand the benefits of standardization
5. Startups should reflect on how different negotiation would’ve changed their outcome (e.g. if they knew they were going to raise under the cap, would they have been more focused on the discount rate and less on the cap?)



## Setup:

7 groups (6x2 startups, 1x3 startups)



### Needs:

* Backstory on the company
* Financial Projections
* Growth Plan
* Role specific instructions (one for investors and one for startups)



## Pre-Scenario Activities

1. Startups and Investors will negotiate an acceptable discount and cap for their fictional company



## Potential scenarios:

1. Things go well - raise above the cap
2. Things go poorly - raise well below the cap
3. Things go poorly but market takes off - raise well above the cap
4. Things go well, but market seizes up - raise below the cap



## Post-Scenario Activities

1. Startups should calculate their ownership percentage based on their scenario, using online tool



## Follow-up:

* Startups should get a report of what would’ve happened in all scenarios
* Startups should share their negotiation results, scenario, and ownership outcome with the group
* Facilitator should compare negotiated cap and resultant ownership percentage post-raise
* Facilitator should encourage discussion of how groups would’ve approached negotiations had they known what the future held
